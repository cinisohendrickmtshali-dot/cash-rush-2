# 💰 Cash Rush — Business Simulator

A free business simulation game for South Africa. Build a small store from R500 and grow your empire.

## 🎮 Gameplay

1. Buy stock from your supplier
2. Set your selling prices
3. Open the store — serve customers
4. Review your daily profit report
5. Reinvest and grow

## ✨ Features

- 📦 25 products across 6 categories
- 🛒 4 customer types with unique behaviour
- 🎲 22 random events (loadshedding, payday, trends)
- 📊 Detailed daily reports
- 🏆 8 achievements to unlock
- 📈 Difficulty that ramps as you grow
- 💾 Auto-save progress
- 📱 Works offline (PWA)

## 🇿🇦 Made for South Africa

Real Rand pricing, loadshedding events, and local products you'd find in every spaza shop.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JavaScript
- LocalStorage for saves
- Service Worker for offline
- PWA-ready
